A Journey Through North, Centre and South

The group flies from Vancouver and travels through three regions of Vietnam — each stop a church, a shrine, a memory.

  1. Days 1–2 · January 8–9, 2028

    Vancouver → Taipei → Hanoi

    International flight — the group departs on the evening of January 8, connects through Taipei and arrives in Hanoi on the evening of January 9.

  2. Day 3 · January 10, 2028

    Hanoi

    St. Joseph’s Cathedral, Hoàn Kiếm Lake and the Old Quarter — a day to greet the homeland.

  3. Days 4–5 · January 11–12, 2028

    Ninh Bình — Phát Diệm · Tràng An

    Anniversary Mass for Fr. Thomas’s ordination at Phát Diệm Cathedral; a day of silence at Châu Sơn Monastery.

  4. Days 6–7 · January 13–14, 2028

    Huế — La Vang Shrine

    Returning to Mother Mary: all of January 14 on pilgrimage at the National Marian Center.

  5. Days 8–9 · January 15–16, 2028

    Da Nang — Hội An

    The Imperial City of Huế, the Hải Vân Pass, Bà Nà Hills and the ancient town of Hội An.

  6. Day 10 · January 17, 2028

    Vũng Tàu

    The Christ the King statue and Mass at the Shrine of Our Lady of Bãi Dâu.

  7. Days 11–12 · January 18–19, 2028

    Saigon

    Our Lady of Fatima in Bình Triệu, Notre-Dame Cathedral and St. Francis Xavier Church in Chợ Lớn.

  8. Days 13–14 · January 20–21, 2028

    Saigon → Taipei → Vancouver

    Farewell to the homeland — the group arrives back in Vancouver on the evening of January 21.

Preparing for the Pilgrimage

What each member needs to take care of before January 8, 2028 — so we leave in peace and return full of blessings.

Documents & Visa

  • Canadian passport valid for at least 6 months after the return date (through July 2028).
  • Canadian passport holders need a Vietnam e-visa before departure. This trip enters Vietnam once, so a single-entry visa is enough: the official fee is US$25 (multiple-entry: US$50), valid up to 90 days, processed in 3–5 working days. Visa rules can change — check the official site again closer to departure.
  • Apply online yourself at the official portal, evisa.gov.vn — from home, no consulate visit needed.
  • Carry copies of your passport and e-visa (printed and saved on your phone).

Insurance & Health

  • Buy travel insurance that covers medical costs and repatriation — especially important because the group includes many older travellers.
  • Bring enough of your daily medication for all 14 days, in carry-on luggage, with a prescription in English.
  • Anyone with a medical condition should have a check-up and speak to their doctor before travelling.
  • Drink bottled water, and pack a few common remedies (colds, upset stomach).

Luggage

  • Northern Vietnam is cool in January (about 15–18°C, with possible drizzle): bring a warm layer and a light waterproof jacket.
  • Central and southern Vietnam are dry and pleasant: light, cool clothing for all 14 days.
  • Comfortable walking shoes — the group will walk a lot at shrines and in the old towns.
  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen; swimwear if you want to swim at Mỹ Khê Beach (Day 8).
  • Bring a universal plug adapter to be safe.

Spiritual Preparation

  • Bring your Rosary and a personal prayer book.
  • Go to Confession before departure; the group will celebrate Mass together on each day of pilgrimage.
  • Prepare a personal intention in your heart to offer to Our Lady of La Vang (January 14).
  • Set out with a pilgrim’s heart — slowing down, listening and giving thanks.

Practical information

Weather across three regions

The north is cool (15–18°C, possible drizzle); the centre and south are dry and sunny.

Currency

The Vietnamese đồng is the local currency. Bring a little US cash for tips and small purchases; hotels and larger venues accept credit cards.

Staying connected

Buy an eSIM or a Vietnamese travel SIM at the airport for mobile data throughout the trip.

Time difference

Vietnam is 15 hours ahead of Vancouver (winter time) — plan to use the first day to adjust.
