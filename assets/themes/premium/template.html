02 — About

A life of ministry

A priest. A listener.
A companion on the journey of faith.

Fr. Thomas Truong is a Catholic priest serving at St. Francis Xavier Parish in Vancouver, Canada. His ministry brings together preaching, Catholic formation, and pastoral care — including ministry among Chinese-speaking communities in Cantonese and Mandarin.

From the altar to the classroom, from retreats to everyday conversations, every ministry begins with listening and leads back to the Gospel.
